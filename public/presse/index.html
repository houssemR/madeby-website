In English

Craft MadeBy in short.

Craft MadeBy is a Danish app that lets knitters, crocheters and other makers document a project step by step as they make it: a photo and a note for every step, a row counter (also as a home-screen widget), the hours actually spent, and a project book as a PDF. Finished pieces can earn a Certified Handmade seal, awarded by the app's expert panel from the process recorded along the way.

From 1 to 24 December 2026 the app runs two free Christmas challenges, a knit-along and a crochet-along, in which participants are seated in small groups of five. The app is free and ad-free, in 12 languages, on iPhone, iPad and Android. Premium costs DKK 49 a month with a 14-day free trial.
